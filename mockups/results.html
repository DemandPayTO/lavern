Re: Termination of Jane Smith

We are counsel for Jane Smith in connection with the termination of her employment with Acme Corporation on June 1, 2026. ✓

Ms. Smith was employed for 8.3 years as a Marketing Manager. Under the Employment Standards Act, 2000, ss. 57–58, she is entitled to a minimum of 8 weeks' notice of termination. ✓

The termination provision in Ms. Smith's 2018 employment agreement purports to limit her entitlements to the statutory minimum. That provision is unenforceable. Its "just cause" language fails to meet the ESA "wilful misconduct" standard, and under Waksdale v Swegon Power & Cooling Systems Inc., 2020 ONCA 391, the entire termination clause is void. ✓

Ms. Smith is therefore entitled to common law reasonable notice. Applying the Bardal factors — her age, 8.3 years of service, the managerial character of her role, and the limited availability of comparable employment ⚠ — reasonable notice falls in the range of 10 to 14 months, or approximately $79,167 to $110,833. ✓

We further note that Ms. Smith's termination followed within days of a disability accommodation request, raising potential claims under the Human Rights Code and for aggravated damages per Honda Canada Inc. v Keays, 2008 SCC 39. ✓

We invite Acme Corporation to respond with a reasonable severance proposal within 14 days of the date of this letter, failing which we are instructed to commence proceedings in the Superior Court of Justice. ✓

Yours truly,
