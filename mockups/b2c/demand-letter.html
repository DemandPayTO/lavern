Demand Letter — Smith v Acme Corporation✓ Ready · Ontario employment law
WITHOUT PREJUDICE

June 25, 2026

Human Resources Department
Acme Corporation

Re: Termination of Jane Smith

I am writing in connection with the termination of my employment with Acme Corporation on June 1, 2026, after 8.3 years of service as a Marketing Manager.

Under the Employment Standards Act, 2000, I am entitled to a minimum of 8 weeks' notice. However, the termination provision in my employment agreement is unenforceable: its "just cause" language does not meet the ESA "wilful misconduct" standard, and under Waksdale v Swegon Power & Cooling Systems Inc., 2020 ONCA 391, the entire termination clause is void.

I am therefore entitled to common law reasonable notice. Applying the Bardal factors — my age, length of service, and managerial role — reasonable notice falls in the range of 6 to 10 months.

The manner of my dismissal — terminated the same day, without explanation — may also give rise to aggravated damages per Honda Canada Inc. v Keays, 2008 SCC 39.

Accordingly, I demand payment equivalent to $78,000. I ask that Acme Corporation respond within 14 days, failing which I am prepared to pursue my claim in the Superior Court of Justice.

Yours truly,

Jane Smith
