What this means

In Ontario, when you're dismissed without cause, you're entitled to reasonable notice — or pay in place of it. The Employment Standards Act sets the floor, but common law usually entitles you to much more.

Your common law notice isn't a fixed number. It's estimated using the Bardal factors — your age, length of service, the type of role, and how hard it'll be to find comparable work.

Because your employment contract's termination clause may be unenforceable under Waksdale, you likely default to the full common law range rather than the contract's minimum.

iThis is an estimate, not a guarantee. Your actual entitlement depends on the full facts. A demand letter is the usual next step to put your position to the employer.
