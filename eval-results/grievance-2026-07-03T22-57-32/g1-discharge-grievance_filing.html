Statement of Grievance

The Union grieves that the Employer violated Article 5.02 and Article 11 of the Collective Agreement, and any other relevant articles of the Collective Agreement, and any applicable statute including the Human Rights Code and the Employment Standards Act, 2000, when it discharged the Grievor on June 24, 2026, without just cause.

Articles Violated

Article 5.02 (just cause), Article 11 (grievance procedure and union representation), and any other relevant articles of the Collective Agreement, and any applicable statute including the Human Rights Code and the Employment Standards Act, 2000.

Particulars

The Grievor was discharged on June 24, 2026, for allegedly falsifying a lockout verification record on one occasion. The discharge letter is dated June 26, 2026. The Grievor has eighteen years of service with the Employer. The discharge meeting lasted approximately four minutes. The Employer conducted no investigation prior to imposing discipline. The Grievor was not afforded union representation at the disciplinary meeting. The Grievor's only prior discipline was a written warning in 2019 for late reporting, which was not grieved and has expired pursuant to the sunset clause in the Collective Agreement (24 months).

Remedy Requested

The Union requests that the Grievor be reinstated immediately to his former position with full redress, including but not limited to:

  • Full compensation for all lost wages, benefits, pension contributions, and seniority from the date of discharge to the date of reinstatement;
  • Removal of all reference to the discharge from the Grievor's employment record;
  • Interest on all monetary amounts owing;
  • Such other relief as is just and appropriate in the circumstances.

The Union estimates back pay and benefits to the date of this grievance at approximately $96,000.00, and continuing.

The Union requests that the Grievor be made whole in all respects.
