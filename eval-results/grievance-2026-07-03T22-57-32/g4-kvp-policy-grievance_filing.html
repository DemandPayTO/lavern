# POLICY GRIEVANCE **Grievance No.:** [To be assigned] **Date of Filing:** July 5, 2026 **Union:** UFCW Local 175 **Employer:** Norfolk Cold Storage Ltd **Workplace:** [To be specified] **Type:** Union Policy Grievance --- ## STATEMENT OF GRIEVANCE The Union grieves that the Employer violated the Collective Agreement, including but not limited to the management rights article, the recognition clause, and any other relevant articles of the Collective Agreement, and any applicable statute including the *Human Rights Code* and the *Employment Standards Act, 2000*, when it unilaterally introduced a mandatory fingerprint biometric time clock system on or about June 20, 2026, with discipline up to and including discharge for refusal to comply, without prior notice to or consultation with the Union. --- ## ARTICLES VIOLATED The Employer has violated the following provisions of the 2025–2028 Collective Agreement: - **Article 6** (Grievance Procedure) - Any article addressing management rights and their limits - Any article addressing recognition of the Union and bargaining unit work - Any article addressing discipline and discharge - Any article addressing hours of work and timekeeping - Any article addressing privacy and personal information - **And any other relevant articles of the Collective Agreement** The Employer has further violated applicable statutes, including but not limited to the *Human Rights Code*, the *Employment Standards Act, 2000*, the *Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act*, and the *Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act*. --- ## PARTICULARS On or about June 20, 2026, the Employer posted a notice on a lunchroom wall announcing the immediate implementation of a mandatory fingerprint biometric time clock system. The policy requires all bargaining unit members to provide fingerprint biometric data for timekeeping purposes and threatens discipline up to and including discharge for refusal to comply. The Employer took this action unilaterally, without any prior notice to or consultation with the Union. The policy constitutes a fundamental change to terms and conditions of employment affecting privacy, personal security, and the employment relationship. The policy is unreasonable, unnecessary, and violates the collective agreement and applicable law. --- ## REMEDY REQUESTED The Union requests the following remedies: 1. A declaration that the Employer's unilateral implementation of the mandatory fingerprint biometric time clock policy violates the Collective Agreement and applicable law; 2. An order that the Employer immediately rescind the policy in its entirety; 3. An order that the Employer immediately cease and desist from implementing or enforcing the policy; 4. An order that the Employer destroy all biometric data collected pursuant to the policy; 5. An order that the Employer expunge any and all discipline issued to bargaining unit members for refusal to comply with the policy; 6. A declaration that the Employer is prohibited from unilaterally implementing similar policies affecting terms and conditions of employment without prior notice to and consultation with the Union; 7. Full redress for all affected bargaining unit members, including compensation for any losses suffered as a result of the Employer's conduct; 8. Such other relief as the arbitrator deems just and appropriate in the circumstances. --- ## UNION REPRESENTATIVE **Signature:** _________________________________ **Name:** Jordan Whitfield, Whitfield Labour Law **Date:** _________________________________ --- ## EMPLOYER ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF RECEIPT **Received by:** _________________________________ **Title:** _________________________________ **Date:** _________________________________ **Time:** _________________________________ --- **DRAFT PREPARED BY:** Jordan Whitfield, Whitfield Labour Law **NOTE:** This is a draft grievance for review by union counsel or the responsible labour relations officer before filing.
