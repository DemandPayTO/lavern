Facts Remaining in Dispute

The following facts are NOT agreed and remain in dispute between the parties. Evidence will be required on these matters:

  • The events that occurred on June 24, 2026, including what the Grievor did or did not do with respect to any lockout verification record.
  • Whether the Grievor falsified any safety documentation.
  • Whether the Employer conducted any investigation prior to imposing discipline, and if so, the nature and adequacy of that investigation.
  • Whether the Grievor was afforded procedural fairness in the investigation and disciplinary process.
  • Whether the Grievor was given an opportunity to respond to the allegations against her prior to the imposition of discipline.
  • The characterization and weight to be given to the written warning issued in 2019, including whether it remains on the Grievor's record and whether it is relevant to the current discipline.
  • Whether the written warning issued in 2019 has been removed from the Grievor's record pursuant to any sunset clause in the Collective Agreement.
  • Whether the Employer had just cause to discipline the Grievor.
  • If the Employer had just cause to impose some discipline, whether discharge was an appropriate penalty in all the circumstances.
  • Whether the grievance was filed within the time limits set out in Article 11 of the Collective Agreement.
  • If the grievance was filed outside the mandatory time limits, whether the time limits should be extended and on what basis.
  • The appropriate remedy, including the quantum of back pay, benefits, and other compensation owing to the Grievor.
  • Whether the Grievor made reasonable efforts to mitigate her losses following discharge.
