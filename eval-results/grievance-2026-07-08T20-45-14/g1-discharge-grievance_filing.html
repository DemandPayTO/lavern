Statement of Grievance

The Union grieves that the Employer violated Articles 5.02, 11, and any other relevant articles of the Collective Agreement, and any applicable statute including the Human Rights Code and the Employment Standards Act, 2000, when it discharged the Grievor on June 26, 2026, without just cause.

Articles Violated

The Employer violated Article 5.02 (just cause), Article 11 (grievance procedure and representation rights), and any other relevant articles of the Collective Agreement, including but not limited to those governing discipline, seniority, and fair treatment.

Particulars

The Grievor, a millwright with 18 years of service, was discharged by letter dated June 26, 2026, for allegedly falsifying a lockout verification record on one occasion on June 24, 2026. The disciplinary meeting lasted four minutes. The Grievor was not afforded union representation at the disciplinary meeting. No proper investigation was conducted before the discipline was imposed. The Grievor's prior discipline consists of one written warning in 2019 for late reporting, which was not grieved and which has expired under the 24-month sunset clause in the Collective Agreement. The discharge is excessive, unjust, and procedurally defective.

Remedy Requested

The Union requests that the Grievor be reinstated immediately to her former position with full compensation for all lost wages, benefits, pension contributions, and seniority, without loss of any rights or privileges under the Collective Agreement. The Union further requests that the discharge be expunged from the Grievor's employment record, and such other relief as is just, including interest on all monetary awards. The Grievor is to be made whole in all respects.
