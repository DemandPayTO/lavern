Type of Grievance

Union Policy Grievance

Statement of Grievance

The Union grieves that the Employer violated the Collective Agreement, including but not limited to Article 6 (Grievance Procedure) and the management rights article, and any other relevant articles of the Collective Agreement, and any applicable statute including the Human Rights Code and the Employment Standards Act, 2000, when it unilaterally introduced a mandatory fingerprint biometric time clock policy on or about June 20, 2026, requiring employees to submit biometric data and threatening discipline up to and including discharge for refusal to comply, without prior notice to or consultation with the Union.

Particulars

On or about June 20, 2026, the Employer posted a policy on a lunchroom wall mandating that all bargaining unit employees use fingerprint biometric time clocks for attendance recording. The policy states that refusal to comply will result in progressive discipline up to and including discharge. The Employer characterized this measure as a time theft prevention policy. The Employer did not provide advance notice to the Union, did not consult with the Union, and did not negotiate the introduction of this policy. The policy is unreasonable, violates employees' privacy rights, and was implemented in breach of the Collective Agreement and applicable law.

Articles Violated

Article 6 (Grievance Procedure), the management rights article (as limited by the requirement of reasonableness and good faith), any articles concerning workplace rules and employee rights, and any other relevant articles of the Collective Agreement, together with any applicable statute including the Human Rights Code, the Employment Standards Act, 2000, and the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act.

Remedy Requested

The Union requests that the Employer:

  • Immediately rescind the mandatory fingerprint biometric time clock policy;
  • Cease and desist from requiring employees to provide biometric data as a condition of employment;
  • Destroy all biometric data collected pursuant to the policy;
  • Provide written confirmation to the Union that no employee will be disciplined for refusing to comply with the policy;
  • Provide the Union with full redress, including but not limited to a declaration that the policy is null and void;
  • Make whole all affected employees in all respects; and
  • Such other relief as is just and appropriate in the circumstances.
