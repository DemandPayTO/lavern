AGREED STATEMENT OF FACTS (PROPOSED)

The parties agree that the following facts are not in dispute for the purposes of this arbitration. This agreement is without prejudice to the positions of the parties on the merits of the grievance and is made solely to narrow the issues and expedite the hearing.

The Parties

  1. The Employer operates a mill in Cornwall, Ontario.
  2. The Union is the exclusive bargaining agent for a unit of employees at the Cornwall mill that includes the classification of Millwright, Maintenance.
  3. The parties are bound by a collective agreement with a term from 2023 to 2026 (the "Collective Agreement") (Tab [1]).

The Employment

  1. The Grievor, Claude Boisvert, was employed by the Employer as a Millwright in the Maintenance department.
  2. The Grievor's seniority date was March 17, 2008.
  3. At the time of his discharge, the Grievor had approximately 18 years of service with the Employer.
  4. [CONFIRM: The Grievor's hourly wage rate at the time of discharge.]

The Collective Agreement Provisions

  1. Article 5.02 of the Collective Agreement provides that the Employer shall not discipline or discharge an employee except for just cause (Tab [2]).
  2. Article 11 of the Collective Agreement sets out the grievance and arbitration procedure (Tab [3]).
  3. Article 11 provides that an employee who believes they have been unjustly disciplined or discharged shall file a grievance within ten (10) working days of the date of the discipline or discharge.
  4. Article 11 states that the time limits for filing grievances are mandatory.
  5. [CONFIRM: The Collective Agreement contains a sunset clause at Article [x] providing that disciplinary records are removed from an employee's file after 24 months.]

Prior Disciplinary Record

  1. The Grievor received a written warning dated [CONFIRM: date] in 2019 for late reporting (Tab [4]).
  2. The Grievor did not grieve the 2019 written warning.
  3. [CONFIRM: No other discipline appears in the Grievor's personnel file.]

The Incident and Investigation

  1. On June 24, 2026, an incident occurred involving the Grievor and a lockout verification record.
  2. [CONFIRM: The Grievor attended a meeting with management on or about June 24 or 25, 2026, concerning the incident.]

The Disciplinary Meeting

  1. The Employer held a disciplinary meeting with the Grievor on or about June 24 or 26, 2026.
  2. The disciplinary meeting lasted approximately four minutes.
  3. Devon Price, a Union steward, was present at the disciplinary meeting.

The Discharge

  1. By letter dated June 26, 2026, the Employer discharged the Grievor (the "Discharge Letter") (Tab [5]).
  2. The Discharge Letter stated that the Grievor was discharged for falsification of safety documentation.
  3. The Discharge Letter was provided to the Grievor on June 26, 2026.
  4. [CONFIRM: A copy of the Discharge Letter was provided to the Union on June 26, 2026, or shortly thereafter.]

The Grievance and Its Procedure

  1. As of the date of this proposed Agreed Statement of Facts, no grievance has been filed concerning the Grievor's discharge.
  2. Calculated from June 26, 2026, the ten (10) working day deadline for filing a grievance under Article 11 would expire on July 8, 2026.

Witnesses

Remedy Sought

  1. The Union seeks the Grievor's reinstatement to employment with full compensation, including back pay, benefits, and seniority, and removal of the discharge from his record.
  2. [CONFIRM: The Grievor has not obtained alternative employment since his discharge, or if he has, particulars of mitigation earnings.]
  3. [CONFIRM: The approximate value of back pay claimed, calculated from June 26, 2026, to the date of hearing, is $96,000, subject to adjustment for the actual hearing date and mitigation.]

Facts Remaining in Dispute

The following facts are not agreed and remain in dispute between the parties:

  • Whether the Grievor falsified a lockout verification record on June 24, 2026.
  • The circumstances surrounding the completion of the lockout verification record on June 24, 2026.
  • Whether the Employer conducted a fair and thorough investigation before imposing discipline.
  • Whether the Grievor was provided with adequate notice of the allegations against him before the disciplinary meeting.
  • Whether the Grievor was provided with an adequate opportunity to respond to the allegations.
  • Whether the Employer had just cause to discipline the Grievor.
  • Whether the Employer had just cause to discharge the Grievor.
  • The characterization and weight to be given to the 2019 written warning, including whether it had sunset under the Collective Agreement at the time of the discharge.
  • Whether the 2019 written warning is properly part of the Grievor's disciplinary record for the purpose of assessing progressive discipline.
  • Whether the Employer's disciplinary process complied with the procedural requirements of the Collective Agreement and the principles of procedural fairness.
  • Whether the penalty of discharge was excessive in all the circumstances.
  • The appropriate remedy, if any.
