# GRIEVANCE **Grievance Number:** [To be assigned] **Date of Filing:** July 7, 2026 **Union:** IAM Local 235 **Employer:** Trillium Paper Products Inc (Cornwall mill) **Workplace:** Cornwall mill --- ## GRIEVOR **Name:** Claude Boisvert **Classification:** Millwright, Maintenance **Seniority Date:** March 17, 2008 --- ## STATEMENT OF GRIEVANCE The Union grieves that the Employer violated Articles 5.02 and 11 of the Collective Agreement, and any other relevant articles of the Collective Agreement, and any applicable statute including the *Human Rights Code* and the *Employment Standards Act, 2000*, when it discharged Claude Boisvert without just cause on June 26, 2026. --- ## ARTICLES VIOLATED The Employer violated the following provisions of the 2023–2026 Collective Agreement: - **Article 5.02** (Just Cause) - **Article 11** (Grievance Procedure) - Any other relevant articles of the Collective Agreement The Employer also violated any applicable statute, including but not limited to the *Human Rights Code*, R.S.O. 1990, c. H.19, and the *Employment Standards Act, 2000*, S.O. 2000, c. 41. --- ## PARTICULARS The Grievor was discharged by letter dated June 26, 2026, for allegedly falsifying a lockout verification record on one occasion. The Employer alleged falsification of safety documentation as the grounds for discharge. The Grievor has 18 years of service with the Employer, with a seniority date of March 17, 2008. His prior discipline consists of one written warning in 2019 for late reporting, which was not grieved and which has expired pursuant to the sunset clause in the Collective Agreement (24 months). The disciplinary meeting lasted four minutes. The Grievor was not provided with union representation at the disciplinary meeting. The Employer conducted no investigation before imposing discipline. The discharge was without just cause and constitutes a breach of the Collective Agreement. --- ## REMEDY REQUESTED The Union requests the following remedies: 1. That the Grievor be reinstated to his position as Millwright, Maintenance, immediately and without loss of seniority, benefits, or other rights under the Collective Agreement; 2. That the Grievor be made whole in all respects, including but not limited to full compensation for all lost wages, benefits, pension contributions, and other compensation from the date of discharge to the date of reinstatement; 3. That the discharge letter dated June 26, 2026, and all references to this matter be removed from the Grievor's employment record; 4. That the Employer cease and desist from violating the Collective Agreement in this manner; 5. That the Grievor be compensated for any damages, losses, or expenses incurred as a result of the Employer's breach of the Collective Agreement; 6. Such other relief as is just and appropriate in the circumstances. --- ## SIGNATURES **Grievor:** _________________________________ Claude Boisvert Date: _____________________________ **Union Representative:** _________________________________ [Name and Title] Date: _____________________________ --- ## EMPLOYER ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF RECEIPT Received by: _________________________________ Title: _________________________________ Date: _____________________________ --- **DRAFT PREPARED BY:** Jordan Whitfield Whitfield Labour Law **NOTE:** This is a draft grievance for review by union counsel or the responsible labour relations officer before filing.
