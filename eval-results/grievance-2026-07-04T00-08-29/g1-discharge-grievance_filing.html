Statement of Grievance

The Union grieves that the Employer violated Articles 5.02, 11, and any other relevant articles of the Collective Agreement, and any applicable statute including the Human Rights Code and the Employment Standards Act, 2000, when it discharged the grievor on June 24, 2026, without just cause.

Particulars

The grievor, an employee with 18 years of seniority, was discharged on June 24, 2026, for allegedly falsifying a lockout verification record on one occasion. The Employer issued a letter of discharge dated June 26, 2026, citing falsification of safety documentation as the stated grounds.

The discharge meeting lasted approximately four minutes. The grievor was not afforded union representation at the disciplinary meeting. The Employer conducted no investigation before imposing discipline.

The grievor's prior discipline consists of one written warning in 2019 for late reporting, which was not grieved. That warning is subject to a 24-month sunset clause and is no longer active on the grievor's record.

Articles Violated

The Employer violated the following provisions of the Collective Agreement:

  • Article 5.02 (just cause for discipline and discharge)
  • Article 11 (grievance procedure)
  • Any and all other relevant articles of the Collective Agreement
  • Any applicable statute, including but not limited to the Human Rights Code, R.S.O. 1990, c. H.19, and the Employment Standards Act, 2000, S.O. 2000, c. 41

Remedy Requested

The Union requests the following remedies:

  • Immediate reinstatement of the grievor to his former position without loss of seniority, rights, or privileges
  • Full compensation for all lost wages, benefits, pension contributions, and other emoluments from the date of discharge to the date of reinstatement
  • Removal of the discharge and all references thereto from the grievor's employment record
  • Interest on all monetary awards at the applicable legal rate
  • A declaration that the Employer violated the Collective Agreement and applicable law
  • Such other relief as is just and appropriate in the circumstances, including full redress and compensation to make the grievor whole in all respects
