# POLICY GRIEVANCE **Grievance No.:** [To be assigned] **Date of Filing:** July 5, 2026 **Union:** UFCW Local 175 **Employer:** Norfolk Cold Storage Ltd **Workplace:** [To be specified] **Collective Agreement:** 2025–2028 Collective Agreement --- ## GRIEVOR This is a **Union Policy Grievance** filed on behalf of all bargaining unit members. --- ## STATEMENT OF GRIEVANCE The Union grieves that the Employer violated Articles 6 and [management rights article], and any other relevant articles of the Collective Agreement, and any applicable statute including the Human Rights Code and the Employment Standards Act, 2000, when it unilaterally introduced a mandatory fingerprint biometric time clock policy on or about June 20, 2026, requiring employees to submit biometric data and threatening discipline up to and including discharge for refusal, without prior notice to or consultation with the Union. --- ## ARTICLES VIOLATED The Employer has violated the following provisions of the Collective Agreement: - **Article 6** (Grievance Procedure) - **[Management Rights Article]** (exercised unreasonably and in bad faith) - **Any article concerning employee privacy, dignity, and working conditions** - **The recognition clause** - **Any and all other relevant articles of the Collective Agreement** - **The Human Rights Code, R.S.O. 1990, c. H.19** - **The Employment Standards Act, 2000, S.O. 2000, c. 41** - **Any other applicable legislation** --- ## PARTICULARS On or about June 20, 2026, the Employer posted a notice on a lunchroom wall announcing the immediate implementation of mandatory fingerprint biometric time clocks throughout the workplace. The policy requires all bargaining unit members to provide fingerprint biometric data for timekeeping purposes and threatens progressive discipline up to and including discharge for employees who refuse to comply. The Employer took this action unilaterally, without any prior notice to the Union and without any consultation or negotiation. The policy constitutes an unreasonable exercise of management rights, violates employees' reasonable expectations of privacy and dignity at work, and fundamentally alters terms and conditions of employment that are subject to collective bargaining. The policy fails to meet the requirements for valid unilateral employer rules as it is unreasonable, was not brought to the attention of employees in a manner consistent with proper notice, is inconsistent with the Collective Agreement's framework of rights and obligations, and was not consistently enforced from the outset given the lack of proper implementation procedures. This grievance is filed within the time limits set out in Article 6 of the Collective Agreement. --- ## REMEDY REQUESTED The Union requests the following remedies: 1. A declaration that the Employer's unilateral implementation of the mandatory fingerprint biometric time clock policy violates the Collective Agreement and applicable law; 2. An order requiring the Employer to immediately rescind and cease implementation of the mandatory fingerprint biometric time clock policy; 3. An order requiring the Employer to immediately destroy all biometric data collected from bargaining unit members pursuant to the impugned policy; 4. An order requiring the Employer to cease and desist from implementing any similar biometric data collection policies without proper notice to and consultation with the Union; 5. An order requiring the Employer to rescind any discipline issued to bargaining unit members for refusing to comply with the impugned policy, and to make whole any affected employees for all losses suffered; 6. A declaration that the Employer must consult with the Union before implementing any future policies that materially affect terms and conditions of employment; 7. Full redress for all bargaining unit members affected by the Employer's conduct, including compensation for any invasion of privacy, loss of dignity, and breach of their rights under the Collective Agreement; 8. Such other relief as the arbitrator deems just and appropriate in all the circumstances. --- ## SIGNATURES **For the Union:** \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ Union Representative UFCW Local 175 Date: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ --- **Employer Acknowledgment of Receipt:** \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ Employer Representative Norfolk Cold Storage Ltd Date: \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ --- **DRAFT PREPARED BY:** Jordan Whitfield Whitfield Labour Law **NOTE:** This is a draft grievance for review and approval before filing.
